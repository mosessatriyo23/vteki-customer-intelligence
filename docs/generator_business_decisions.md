# Generator business decisions (proposed)

- **Customer dates:** `join_date` is defined as the first business relationship date in this synthetic dataset, not merely CRM registration. Transactions, app events, cases, consent capture, visits, and membership join/renewal cannot precede it.
- **Membership:** a 180-day contractual period and 0.65 renewal probability are **PROPOSED** simulation assumptions, not asserted business policy. `churn90` is a separate 90-day activity outcome; it is not membership expiry. `renewal60` measures a 60-day future window. Contract expiry may extend beyond observation because it is known contract metadata; renewal events may not.
- **Campaigns:** a **PROPOSED** 10% randomized untreated control should be assigned before campaign exposure and persisted as an observable assignment record. Current Ramadan/holiday multipliers are calendar effects, **not causal campaign uplift**. Do not infer treatment effects from seasonal peaks or `injected_campaign_effects.json`.
- **Accounting:** `net_amount = gross_amount - discount_amount`. `refund_amount` is zero for completed/cancelled, equals net for refunded, and is strictly between zero and net for partial refund. `actual_revenue` is zero for cancelled/refunded and `net_amount - refund_amount` otherwise. A refund timestamp is not yet modelled, so point-in-time historical refund accounting assumes final refund status is known at transaction time; production training needs versioned refund events.
- **Identity and replay:** source generation preserves typo, formatting, missing field, duplicate source, and shared-phone traps. Canonical email/member code are unique across golden customers. Phone alone must never trigger an automatic merge. Transaction replay truth is evaluation-only and labels never join ground truth; source-only fingerprint dedup remains conservative and may collapse genuinely identical events.
- **Reproducibility:** randomness uses only CPython `random.Random` (MT19937). NumPy, Pandas, and Faker availability cannot alter bytes. Reproducibility is claimed only for the same generator version and Python runtime recorded in the manifest, not across arbitrary runtime/library versions.
- **Compatibility — Moses:** schema v2 adds `refund_amount`, `actual_revenue`, `membership_events.csv`, and `customer_visits.csv`. Use `generator/labels.py` for point-in-time outcomes and only train on rows with `outcome_complete=1`; future columns are labels/assessment values, never features.
- **Compatibility — Christ:** campaign assignment/exposure/conversion contract is documented in `contracts/business_events.md`; generator does not implement Christ's campaign simulator. The proposed control proportion is 10% and must be seeded/persisted before outcomes.
- **Compatibility — James:** ingestion/contracts must add the two transaction columns and two new event tables. Existing columns and filenames remain intact. Schema version is `2.0.0`; migration should reject silent interpretation of old transactions as refund-adjusted.
