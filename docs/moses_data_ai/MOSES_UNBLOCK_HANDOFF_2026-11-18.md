### Delivered pure diagnostic: [`services/data/models/retention_diagnostics.py`](../../services/data/models/retention_diagnostics.py)

`summarize_retention90(labels, segments)` joins the **existing** `churn90` label rows (`label_version="christ-churn90-three-events-dev-1.1"`, `horizon_days=90` only — any other version/horizon raises) to the **existing** `snapshot.py` lifecycle rows by `(golden_customer_id, cutoff_at)`, and groups by `(lifecycle, cutoff_at, rule_version)`. It changes no label, feature, or model version and creates no new `AIScores`/CLV field.

- Denominator is `eligible_complete_count` (fully matured window **and** eligible under the *existing* code's POS-only cohort). `excluded_incomplete_count`/`excluded_ineligible_count` are reported alongside so nobody mistakes the rate for covering the any-event cohort Christ decided in §1.1.
- `cohort_scope="existing_pos_365d_eligible_subset"` is stamped on every row as a standing caveat — this is **not** the any-qualifying-event cohort, and must not be republished as one blended whole-cohort number.
- `retention90_rate` is `None`, never `0`, when the denominator is zero.
- `clv_factor_ready` is always `False`: this is a 90-day descriptive reporting statistic for Christ's review, not the 12-month CLV retention factor in the [retention proposal](RETENTION_METHOD_PROPOSAL_2026-11-17.md); the two estimands are not interchangeable.
- Mismatched label/segment key sets, duplicate keys, wrong label version/horizon, or a non-boolean `eligible`/`outcome_complete` all raise rather than silently coerce.
- Tests: [`tests/data/test_retention_diagnostics.py`](../../tests/data/test_retention_diagnostics.py) (6 cases: exclusion counting, null-not-zero denominator, duplicate/unmatched-key rejection, invalid label rejection, no cross-cutoff/rule-version pooling, empty input). `python -m unittest tests.data.test_retention_diagnostics` passes locally.
